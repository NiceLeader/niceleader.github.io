Fit

Good fit

  • A live or inherited system holds client assets.
  • An audit, launch, incident or architecture decision created urgency.
  • You want findings your engineers can verify and act on.

Not a fit

  • Speculative token promotion or anonymous launch support.
  • An open-ended build with no owner, scope or decision deadline.
  • Any engagement requiring custody of production keys or funds.

How it works

1. Scope
A 30-minute call establishes the system boundary and why the work matters now. You then receive a one-page scope with deliverables, access needs and fixed price.
2. Evidence
I work from read-only code, architecture documentation and operating evidence. Assumptions and unavailable evidence are explicit rather than silently filled in.
3. Handoff
The report, policy and evidence formats are yours. They are written so an engineer, auditor or regulator can use them without me in the room.
